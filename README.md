# Sound drawing

A single page that draws a voice as it sings: open a recording or listen live, and the melody is drawn over the notes the voice actually uses, measured from its qarar (home note).

- **Line**: pitch over the last 12 seconds. Held notes are bold, slides are thin, talk barely shows.
- **Circle**: direction is the note, distance from the centre is time.

Everything runs in the browser. No audio leaves the device.

Live: https://amrxtawfik.github.io/sound-drawing/
